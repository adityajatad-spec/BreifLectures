What gets cut

Every 20 seconds of the lecture gets a label. These are the ones a cut can skip. Explanations, examples and recaps stay.

Logistics Can everyone see my screen? Give me one second.

Mic checks, attendance, waiting for the room, homework reminders.

Repeat So again, just to say it one more time…

The same idea explained again, with nothing new added. It's checked against everything said earlier in the lecture.

Filler Um, okay, so, where was I. Right.

Stalling and setup chatter, in any language.

Tangents and promos Before we start, don't forget to subscribe.

Off-topic stories, sponsor reads and channel plugs. Silence is skipped too.

Questions

Short answers. The longer ones are in the project's docs.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer) on macOS 12 or later. It's sized for 16 GB of memory: the AI model uses about 4.5 GB while it works, and it's unloaded a couple of minutes after.

Do I have to wait for the analysis?

No. The lecture opens about a second after you paste the link, with quick skips for silence and logistics. The AI then works through the lecture from the start, ahead of where you're watching, and its skips switch on part by part.

What if it skips something I needed?

Every skip shows a small "Watch it" button that jumps back and plays that part. You can also turn auto-skip off, or switch to the full lecture at any time.

What about videos with no captions?

It downloads the audio and transcribes it on your Mac with Whisper. This is slower than reading captions, and only happens once per video.

Why do I need Ollama?

Ollama runs the AI model on your Mac. It's free, and BriefLectures walks you through installing it and downloading the model the first time you open it. Without it, you still get a rough offline estimate.

Is it free?

Yes. There's no account and no subscription. Everything runs on your own Mac.
